# Stage 1 — Validate

**Question:** Will people in this market want this specific app enough to pay?

**Inputs:** the Stage 0 pick and riskiest assumption.
**Outputs:** `hypotheses.md`, test assets, `results.md`, `go-no-go.md`.
**Who:** `/validate` with `product-strategist`; the founder runs tests that need money or people.
**Duration:** 3 to 10 days.

## Test ladder (cheapest first)

1. **Review mining** — count competitor reviews in the last 12 months asking for our angle. Free, 1 hour.
2. **Community post** — describe the app; count replies, DMs, "take my money". Free, 1 day.
3. **Landing page smoke test** — waitlist or fake pre-order; $50 to $200 ads or organic. 3 to 7 days.
4. **Interviews** — five users of a competitor, 20 minutes each. 1 week.
5. **Prototype test** — only if the risk is the interaction itself.

## Thresholds that usually mean GO

- Landing page: >= 5% visitor-to-waitlist from a cold audience, or >= 1% fake pre-order clicks.
- Interviews: >= 3 of 5 describe the problem unprompted and name a price they would pay.
- Community: >= 20 substantive replies or >= 5 unsolicited "I'd pay for this".
