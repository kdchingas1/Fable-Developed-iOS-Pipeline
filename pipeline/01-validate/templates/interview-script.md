# Customer interview script — <Product>

Target: current users of <competitor(s)>. 20 minutes. Do not pitch until the end.

1. Tell me about the last time you <did the job>. What did you use?
2. What was frustrating about it?
3. What have you tried to fix that? Did you pay for anything?
4. If you had a magic wand, what would it do?
5. (Show one-line concept.) What would you expect this to cost? Would you pay that?
6. Who else do you know with this problem?
