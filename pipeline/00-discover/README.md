# Stage 0 — Discover

**Question:** Where on the App Store is there real demand, beatable competition, and a
clear way to get paid?

**Inputs:** a theme, category, persona, or nothing at all; founder constraints.
**Outputs:** `brief.md`, `market-scan.md`, `competitors/*.md`, `opportunity-scorecard.md`.
**Who:** `/discover` skill with `market-researcher` agents in parallel.
**Duration:** 1 to 3 days.

## Where to look

- App Store category charts (Top Paid is a strong monetization signal; Top Free shows reach).
- Reviews of the top 10 apps in a category: 1-star and 2-star for unmet needs.
- Apple platform changes in the last two WWDCs: new APIs create windows before incumbents adapt.
- Indie developer revenue reports and "I built X and it makes $Y/month" posts.
- Search: App Store autocomplete, Google Trends, keyword tools (AppFigures, Astro, etc.).

## Scoring model

Weights (sum 100): demand 25, competition weakness 20, monetization clarity 20,
buildability 15, platform tailwind 10, defensibility 10. A pick needs a weighted score of
at least 3.5/5 and no 1 in demand or monetization.
